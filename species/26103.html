Cinnamomum camphora

樟

(L.) J. Presl

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Laurales
Family科Lauraceae
Genus屬Cinnamomum
Species種Cinnamomum camphora

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Tree, up to 30 m tall. All parts strongly camphor-scented.

喬木，高可達 30 米。全株有濃烈樟腦氣味。

Leaves葉

Shape and venation形狀與脈

Alternate. Petiole slender, 2–3 cm, glabrous. Blade ovate-elliptic, 6–12 × 2.5–5.5 cm, base broadly cuneate to rounded, apex acute, margin entire, subleathery. Triplinerved, with domatia in the axils of the secondary and tertiary veins.

互生。葉柄細，2–3 厘米，無毛。葉片卵狀橢圓形，6–12 × 2.5–5.5 厘米，基部闊楔形至圓形，先端急尖，全緣，近革質。離基三出脈，二級脈與三級脈腋內有腺窩。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Yellowish white, ca. 3 mm. Panicles or cymes axillary, 3.5–7 cm. Stamens 9, filaments pubescent.

黃白色，約 3 毫米。圓錐花序或聚傘花序腋生，3.5–7 厘米。雄蕊 9，花絲被毛。

Fruit果

Ovoid or subglobose, 6–8 mm in diam., purplish black when mature.

卵形或近球形，直徑 6–8 毫米，成熟時紫黑色。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Woods near villages. Also cultivated.生境。 村落附近的林中。亦有栽培。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong, and also widely cultivated.狀況。 原生。香港常見，亦廣泛栽培。
  • Distribution. Widely distributed in south to southwest China; Vietnam, Korea and Japan.分布。 中國南至西南廣泛分布；越南、韓國及日本。

Representative photographs代表照片

Source出處
