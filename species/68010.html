Enkianthus quinqueflorus

吊鐘花

Lour.

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1 出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Deciduous or semi-deciduous shrub or trees, 1–3(–10) m tall, glabrous throughout, with whorled branches.

落葉或半落葉灌木或喬木，高 1–3（–10）米，全體無毛，枝輪生。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Leaves crowded at ends of branches; petiole 0.5–1.5 cm. Blade obovate-lanceolate to elliptic, rarely lanceolate, 5–12.5 × 1.8–4 cm, base cuneate to attenuate, apex acuminate, margin entire, thick papery to leathery, shining adaxially.

葉聚生於枝端；葉柄長 0.5–1.5 厘米。葉片倒卵狀披針形至橢圓形，稀披針形，長 5–12.5 厘米，寬 1.8–4 厘米，基部楔形至漸狹，先端漸尖，全緣，厚紙質至革質，上面光亮。

Flowers花

Corolla花冠

Inflorescence terminal, umbellate, 6–8(–16)-flowered, enclosed in imbricate reddish scales in bud. Pedicels 1–2 cm, usually curved downward. Corolla broadly campanulate, ca. 1.2 cm across, pink, red, or white, shortly 5-lobed.

花序頂生，傘形，有花 6–8（–16）朵，芽時為覆瓦狀紅色鱗片所包。花梗長 1–2 厘米，通常下彎。花冠寬鐘狀，直徑約 1.2 厘米，粉紅、紅或白色，淺 5 裂。

Fruit果

Capsule蒴果

Capsule ovoid, 5-angled, ca. 1.2 × 1.0 cm; stalk erect; seeds several to many.

蒴果卵形，有 5 稜，約 1.2 × 1.0 厘米；果梗直立；種子數枚至多數。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1 出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

  • Habitat. Open hillside shrublands, alt. 50–700 m.生境。 開闊山坡灌叢，海拔 50–700 米。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong. Cap. 96.狀況。 原生。香港常見。受《林規》附表保護。
  • Distribution. Fujian, Guangdong, Guangxi, Guizhou, Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Sichuan, Yunnan; Vietnam.分布。 福建、廣東、廣西、貴州、湖北、湖南、江西、四川、雲南；越南。

Representative photographs代表照片

Imbricate green-red bud scales with bronze leaves and an old capsule.覆瓦狀綠紅芽鱗，伴青銅色葉與舊蒴果。
