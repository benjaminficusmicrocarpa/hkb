Species種Liquidambar formosana

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Tree to 30 m tall. Bark grey-brown. Branchlets pubescent or glabrous, slightly lenticellate. Buds ovoid, glossy.

喬木，高可達 30 米。樹皮灰褐色。小枝被毛或無毛，稍具皮孔。芽卵形，有光澤。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Petioles to 12 cm. Blade broadly ovate, palmately 3-lobed and 3-veined, the central lobe longer, base rounded, apex caudate-acuminate, margin glandular-serrate.

葉柄長可達 12 厘米。葉片闊卵形，掌狀 3 裂並具 3 脈，中裂片較長，基部圓形，先端尾狀漸尖，邊緣有腺鋸齒。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Male inflorescence a short spike, several arranged in a raceme. Female inflorescence capitate, 20–40-flowered, the peduncle 3–6 cm.

雄花序為短穗狀，數個排成總狀。雌花序頭狀，有花 20–40 朵，花序柄長 3–6 厘米。

Infructescence果序

Globose, 3–4 cm wide. Capsules with persistent staminodes and styles.

球形，寬 3–4 厘米。蒴果有宿存的退化雄蕊及花柱。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Forests.生境。 森林。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong, and also widely planted.狀況。 原生。香港常見，亦廣泛栽植。
  • Distribution. South, southwest, east and central China; South Korea, Laos and Vietnam.分布。 華南、西南、華東及華中；南韓、老撾及越南。
