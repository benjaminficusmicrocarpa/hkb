Thespesia populnea

恆春黃槿 · 繖楊

(L.) Solander ex Corr.

Home首頁 Species Directory物種目錄 Thespesia populneaBLP 61083

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Malvales
Family科Malvaceae
Genus屬Thespesia
Species種Thespesia populnea

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Evergreen small tree, 6–8 m tall. Branchlets scabrous, densely covered with brown scurfy scales.

常綠小喬木，高 6–8 米。小枝粗糙，密被褐色糠秕狀鱗片。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Petioles 4–10 cm. Blade ovate-cordate, 7–18 × 4.5–11 cm, base cordate, apex long-caudate, margin entire, glabrous above, sparsely scurfy beneath.

葉柄 4–10 厘米。葉片卵狀心形，7–18 × 4.5–11 厘米，基部心形，先端長尾狀，全緣，上面無毛，下面疏被糠秕。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Solitary, axillary. Calyx cup-shaped, about 15 mm across, truncate and 5-toothed. Corolla campanulate, about 5 cm, yellow, with purple blotches at the base inside.

單生，腋生。萼杯狀，直徑約 15 毫米，先端截形並有 5 齒。花冠鐘狀，約 5 厘米，黃色，內面基部有紫色斑塊。

Fruit果

Pyriform, about 5 cm across. Seed triangular-ovoid, striate, lanuginose.

梨形，直徑約 5 厘米。種子三角狀卵形，有條紋，被綿毛。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Sunny places near seashores.生境。 海岸附近向陽處。
  • Status. Native. Tai Tam Reservoir, Tai Po, Butterfly Beach, Lung Kwu Tan, Nam Chung, Luk Keng, Lantau Island, Ma Wan and Po Toi.狀況。 原生。大潭水塘、大埔、蝴蝶灣、龍鼓灘、南涌、鹿頸、大嶼山、馬灣及蒲台。
  • Distribution. Hainan, Guangxi, Guangdong and Taiwan; Indochina, Thailand, India, Sri Lanka, the Philippines and tropical Africa.分布。 海南、廣西、廣東及台灣；中南半島、泰國、印度、斯里蘭卡、菲律賓及熱帶非洲。
