Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Arecales
Family科Arecaceae
Genus屬Phoenix
Species種Phoenix loureiroi

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Small to moderate tree, 1–5 m. Stems solitary or clustered, 25–40 cm in diam., sometimes short and subterranean, rough, with petiole-base remains.

小至中等喬木，高 1–5 米。莖單生或叢生，直徑 25–40 厘米，有時短而埋於地下，粗糙，留有葉柄基部。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Pinnate, to 2 m. Pinnae 35–120, linear, solitary or in groups of 2–3, not in the same plane. Middle pinnae 20–45 × 1–3 cm.

羽狀，長可達 2 米。羽片 35–120 枚，線形，單生或 2–3 枚成組，不在同一平面。中部羽片 20–45 × 1–3 厘米。

Acanthophylls刺狀羽片

About 15 per side of the rachis, on the lower rachis.

葉軸每側約 15 枚，生於下部葉軸。

Flowers花

Inflorescence花序

Branched to one order, with 30–40 rachillae. Peduncles to 50 cm.

1 回分枝，小穗軸 30–40 條。花序梗長可達 50 厘米。

Appearance外觀

Yellowish white. Male flowers: calyx 1–1.5 mm, 3-lobed; petals 3, 4–5 × 1.5–2 mm; stamens 6.

黃白色。雄花：花萼 1–1.5 毫米，3 裂；花瓣 3，4–5 × 1.5–2 毫米；雄蕊 6。

Fruit果

Appearance外觀

Oblong, 1.5–2 cm, black or purplish black.

長圓形，1.5–2 厘米，黑色或紫黑色。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Open forests.生境。 開闊的森林。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong.狀況。 原生。香港常見。
  • Distribution. Hainan, Guangdong, Guangxi, Yunnan, Fujian and Taiwan; Indian Subcontinent, Southeast Asia.分布。 海南、廣東、廣西、雲南、福建及台灣；印度次大陸、東南亞。

Representative photographs代表照片

Solitary stem with petiole-base remains, and a pinnate crown, in open forest.疏林中的單幹，幹上留有葉柄基部，樹冠羽狀。

Source出處

Hong Kong Herbarium & South China Botanical Garden (Eds.). (2011). Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌] (Vol. 4). Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.
