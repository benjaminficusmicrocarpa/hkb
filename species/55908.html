BLP 55908Native原生Euphorbiaceae

Mallotus apelta

白背葉

(Lour.) Müll. Arg.

Plantae/Tracheophyta/angiosperm 被子植物/Magnoliopsida 木蘭綱/mesangiosperms 中生被子植物/eudicots 真雙子葉植物/core eudicots 核心真雙子葉植物/superrosids 超薔薇類/rosids 薔薇類/malvids 錦葵類/Malpighiales 金虎尾目/Euphorbiaceae 大戟科/Mallotus 野桐屬/Mallotus apelta 白背葉

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Shrub or small tree, usually 1–4 m. Branchlets, petioles and the inflorescence are densely yellowish stellate-hairy, with scattered orange glands.

灌木或小喬木，通常高 1–4 米。小枝、葉柄和花序密被黃褐色星狀毛，並散生橙色腺體。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Alternate, ovate or broadly ovate, 6–16(–25) cm. Base truncate or slightly cordate, with two brown glands where the petiole joins. Apex acute or acuminate. Margin sparsely serrate. Lower surface white with stellate hairs.

互生，卵形或闊卵形，6–16（–25）厘米。基部截形或稍心形，葉柄着生處有褐色腺體 2 枚。先端急尖或漸尖。邊緣疏生鋸齒。下面白色，被星狀毛。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Male spikes 15–30 cm. Male flowers have 50–75 stamens. Female flowers are solitary in each bract, with 3–4 styles.

雄花序穗狀，長 15–30 厘米。雄花有雄蕊 50–75 枚。雌花單生於苞片內，花柱 3–4。

Fruit果

Capsule nearly globose, 1–1.3 cm across, stellate-hairy and softly prickly. The prickles are 5–10 mm.

蒴果近球形，直徑 1–1.3 厘米，被星狀毛，並有軟刺。刺長 5–10 毫米。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

  • Habitat. Thin forests and shrubland.生境。 疏林及灌叢。
  • Status. Native.狀況。 原生。
  • Distribution. Southern and central China, from Hainan and Guangdong north to Henan and Shaanxi.分布。 中國南部及中部，自海南、廣東北至河南及陝西。
