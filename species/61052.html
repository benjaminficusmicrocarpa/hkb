Heritiera littoralis

銀葉樹

Aiton

Home首頁 Species Directory物種目錄 Heritiera littoralisBLP 61052

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Malvales
Family科Malvaceae
Genus屬Heritiera
Species種Heritiera littoralis

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Evergreen tree. Branchlets white-lepidote when young.

常綠喬木。幼枝被白色鱗秕。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Petioles 1–2 cm. Leaves leathery, oblong-lanceolate, elliptic or ovate, 10–20 × 5–10 cm. Base obtuse. Apex acute or obtuse. Margin entire. Densely silvery-lepidote abaxially.

葉柄 1–2 厘米。葉革質，長圓狀披針形、橢圓形或卵形，10–20 × 5–10 厘米。基部鈍。先端急尖或鈍。全緣。背面密被銀色鱗秕。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Panicles axillary, many-flowered. Flowers red-brown. Calyx campanulate, 4–6 mm.

圓錐花序腋生，多花。花紅褐色。萼鐘形，4–6 毫米。

Fruit果

Ripe carpels woody, yellow-brown, nut-like, ovoid, ca. 6 × 3.5 cm, with a strong, almost winged keel along the outer edge.

成熟心皮木質，黃褐色，堅果狀，卵形，約 6 × 3.5 厘米，外緣有一條近翅狀的強龍骨。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Seashores.生境。 海岸。
  • Status. Native.狀況。 原生。
  • Distribution. Guangdong, Guangxi, Hainan and Taiwan; Japan, the Philippines, Southeast Asia, East Africa and Australia.分布。 廣東、廣西、海南及台灣；日本、菲律賓、東南亞、東非及澳洲。
