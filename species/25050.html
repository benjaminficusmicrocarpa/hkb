Magnolia championii

香港木蘭

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1 出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Evergreen shrub or small tree. Young twigs are green and hairy. Stipular scars nearly reach the petiole apex.

常綠灌木或小喬木。幼枝綠色，有毛。托葉痕幾乎到達葉柄先端。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Leaves elliptic to narrowly obovate-elliptic, 7–14(–20) × 2–4.5(–6.5) cm, leathery, glossy above, pale and more or less hairy on the midvein beneath.

葉橢圓形至狹倒卵狀橢圓形，長 7–14（–20）厘米，寬 2–4.5（–6.5）厘米，革質，上面亮綠色，下面淺色，中脈多少有毛。

Flowers花

Tepals花被片

Flowers globose, 1.5–2 cm across, very fragrant. Peduncle densely pale-yellow silky. Tepals 9: the outer three pale green, the inner six white and fleshy.

花近球形，直徑 1.5–2 厘米，很香。花梗密被淡黃絹毛。花被片 9：外輪 3 片淺綠色，內輪 6 片白色、肉質。

Ecology生態
