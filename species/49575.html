BLP 49575Moraceae

Artocarpus parvus

桂木

Gagnep.

Plantae/Tracheophyta/angiosperm 被子植物/Magnoliopsida 木蘭綱/mesangiosperms 中生被子植物/eudicots 真雙子葉植物/core eudicots 核心真雙子葉植物/superrosids 超薔薇類/rosids 薔薇類/fabids 豆類/Rosales 薔薇目/Moraceae 桑科/Artocarpus 波羅蜜屬/Artocarpus parvus 桂木

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Tree to 17 m. Bark black to brown, split lengthways. Branchlets wrinkled, with appressed short hairs, soon nearly hairless.

喬木，高可達 17 米。樹皮黑至褐色，縱裂。小枝有皺紋，被貼伏的短毛，很快近無毛。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Distichous, thinly leathery, oblong-elliptic to obovate-elliptic, 7–15 × 3–7 cm. Base cuneate to nearly rounded. Apex with a short or long point. Margin entire or irregularly shallowly toothed. Hairless. Secondary veins 6–10 on each side, prominent above.

二列，薄革質，長圓狀橢圓形至倒卵狀橢圓形，7–15 × 3–7 厘米。基部楔形至近圓。先端有短尖或長尖。全緣或不規則淺齒。無毛。側脈每邊 6–10 條，上面明顯。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Male heads obovoid or oblong, 2.5–12 × 2.7–7 mm. Male flowers with 2–4 perianth lobes.

雄花序頭狀，倒卵形或長圓形，2.5–12 × 2.7–7 毫米。雄花花被裂片 2–4。

Fruit果

Syncarp red when ripe, nearly globose, coarsely hairy.

聚花果成熟時紅色，近球形，被粗毛。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

  • Status. Cultivated. Not native.狀況。 栽培。非原生。
  • Distribution. Hainan, Guangdong, Guangxi, Yunnan and Sichuan; Vietnam, Cambodia and Thailand.分布。 海南、廣東、廣西、雲南及四川；越南、柬埔寨及泰國。
