Dioscorea bulbifera

黃獨

L.

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 4 出處：《香港植物誌》，第四卷

Habit習性

Stem莖

Herbaceous vine. Stem twining to the left, glabrous, bearing bulbils in the leaf axils. Tuber usually solitary, globular to pyriform, 4–10 cm thick.

草質藤本。莖向左纏繞，無毛，葉腋生珠芽。塊莖通常單生，球形至梨形，厚 4–10 厘米。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Leaves alternate, simple, broadly cordate, 5–15(–26) × 4–14(–26) cm, apex caudate-acuminate, glabrous.

葉互生，單葉，寬心形，長 5–15（–26）厘米，寬 4–14（–26）厘米，先端尾狀漸尖，無毛。

Bulbils珠芽

Aerial bulbils氣生珠芽

Round bulbils sit in the leaf axils.

圓形珠芽生於葉腋。

Flowers花

Spikes穗狀花序

Male spikes usually clustered in the leaf axils, drooping, 3–10 cm or longer; perianth purple. Female spikes often two or more together, 20–50 cm.

雄花穗通常簇生於葉腋，下垂，長 3–10 厘米或更長；花被紫色。雌花穗常 2 條或更多並生，長 20–50 厘米。

Fruit果

Capsule蒴果

Capsule of three flat wing-like lobes, 1.5–3 cm, straw-coloured.

蒴果有 3 枚扁平的翅狀裂片，長 1.5–3 厘米，禾稈色。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 4 出處：《香港植物誌》，第四卷

  • Distribution. Widespread in southern and central China; also Korea, Japan, Southeast Asia, India, Africa and Oceania.分布。 中國南部及中部廣布；亦見於朝鮮、日本、東南亞、印度、非洲及大洋洲。
