BLP 76626Native原生Lamiaceae

Callicarpa kochiana

枇杷葉紫珠

Makino

Plantae/Tracheophyta/angiosperm 被子植物/Magnoliopsida 木蘭綱/mesangiosperms 中生被子植物/eudicots 真雙子葉植物/core eudicots 核心真雙子葉植物/superasterids 超菊類/asterids 菊類/lamiids 唇形類/Lamiales 唇形目/Lamiaceae 唇形科/Callicarpa 紫珠屬/Callicarpa kochiana 枇杷葉紫珠

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 3出處：《香港植物誌》，第三卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Shrub, 1–4 m. Branchlets, petioles and the inflorescence are densely covered with yellowish-brown branched stellate hairs.

灌木，高 1–4 米。小枝、葉柄和花序密被黃褐色的分枝星狀毛。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Oblong-lanceolate, elliptic or ovate-elliptic, 12–22 × 4–8 cm. Base cuneate. Apex acuminate or acute. Margin serrate. Upper surface glabrous or slightly hairy on the veins. Lower surface densely yellowish-brown stellate-tomentose, and obscurely yellow-glandular.

長圓狀披針形、橢圓形或卵狀橢圓形，12–22 × 4–8 厘米。基部楔形。先端漸尖或急尖。邊緣有鋸齒。上面無毛或脈上稍被毛。下面密被黃褐色星狀絨毛，兩面有不明顯的黃色腺點。

Venation脈

Lateral veins 10–18 pairs, prominent beneath. Petiole 1–3 cm.

側脈 10–18 對，下面明顯。葉柄 1–3 厘米。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Cymes 3–6 cm across, on a peduncle 1–2 cm. Flowers almost sessile. Calyx tubular, 2–2.5 mm, hairy, with acute teeth. Corolla pink to purple, the lobes densely hairy. Stamens exserted.

聚傘花序寬 3–6 厘米，花序梗 1–2 厘米。花近無柄。萼筒狀，長 2–2.5 毫米，被毛，齒尖。花冠粉紅至紫色，裂片密被毛。雄蕊伸出。

Fruit果

Globose, about 1.5 mm across, nearly enclosed by the persistent calyx.

球形，直徑約 1.5 毫米，幾乎全被宿存的萼包裹。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 3出處：《香港植物誌》，第三卷

  • Habitat. Forest edges and forests in valleys.生境。 林緣及山谷林。
  • Status. Native.狀況。 原生。
  • Distribution. Fujian, Guangdong, Hunan, Jiangxi, Henan, Zhejiang, Taiwan; Japan and Vietnam.分布。 福建、廣東、湖南、江西、河南、浙江、台灣；日本及越南。
