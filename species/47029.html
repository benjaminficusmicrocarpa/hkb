Delonix regia

鳳凰木

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2 出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Tree to 20 m tall, with spreading branches. Bark rough, greyish brown.

喬木，高可達 20 米，枝開展。樹皮粗糙，灰褐色。

Leaves葉

Leaflets小葉

Leaves 20–60 cm. Pinnae 15–20 pairs. Leaflets 10–25 pairs, oblong, 4–8 × 3–4 mm, base oblique, apex obtuse, sericeous on both surfaces.

葉長 20–60 厘米。羽片 15–20 對。小葉 10–25 對，長圓形，4–8 × 3–4 毫米，基部偏斜，先端鈍，兩面被絹毛。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Racemes 20–40 cm. Flowers 7–10 cm across. Petals bright scarlet, mixed with yellow and white patches, suborbicular, 5–7 cm including the stalk. Stamens red.

總狀花序長 20–40 厘米。花直徑 7–10 厘米。花瓣鮮緋紅色，雜有黃斑及白斑，近圓形，連柄長 5–7 厘米。雄蕊紅色。

Pods莢果

30–60 × 3.5–5 cm, blackish brown when mature, the style persistent at the apex. Seeds many, yellow with brown patches.

長 30–60 厘米，寬 3.5–5 厘米，成熟時黑褐色，先端有宿存花柱。種子多數，黃色而有褐斑。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2 出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

  • Habitat. Cultivated.生境。 栽培。
  • Status. Exotic. Cultivated in Hong Kong. Native to Madagascar.狀況。 外來。香港有栽培。原產馬達加斯加。
  • Distribution. Hainan, Guangdong, Guangxi, Yunnan, Fujian and Taiwan (cultivated); native to Madagascar, and widely cultivated in the tropics.分布。 海南、廣東、廣西、雲南、福建及台灣（栽培）；原產馬達加斯加，熱帶地區廣泛栽培。
