Heptapleurum heptaphyllum

鴨腳木

Home首頁 Species Directory物種目錄 Heptapleurum heptaphyllumBLP 82051

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Apiales
Family科Araliaceae
Genus屬Heptapleurum
Species種Heptapleurum heptaphyllum

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Evergreen shrub or tree, up to 10 m. Young parts tawny tomentose, later glabrescent.

常綠灌木或喬木，高可達 10 米。幼時黃褐色絨毛，後漸無毛。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Digitately compound, generally 6–8-foliolate. Leaflets elliptic or ovate-elliptic, 7–17 × 3–7.5 cm, margin entire.

掌狀複葉，通常 6–8 小葉。小葉橢圓形或卵狀橢圓形，7–17 × 3–7.5 厘米，全緣。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Many-flowered umbels in a large terminal panicle. Petals 5.

多花的傘形花序組成大型頂生圓錐花序。花瓣 5。

Fruit果

Globose, 3–4 mm.

球形，直徑 3–4 毫米。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Forests.生境。 森林。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong.狀況。 原生。香港常見。
  • Distribution. Southern China to Japan, the Philippines, Vietnam and India.分布。 華南至日本、菲律賓、越南及印度。

Representative photographs代表照片

Flowering tree with pale bark.開花的樹，樹皮淡色。

Source出處

Hong Kong Herbarium & South China Botanical Garden (Eds.). (2008). Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌] (Vol. 2). Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.
