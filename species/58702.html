Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2 出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Shrub, 1.5–3 m tall.

灌木，高 1.5–3 米。

Indumentum毛被

Stems, branches, petioles and pedicels densely covered with spreading bristles up to 8 mm.

莖、枝、葉柄和花梗密被開展的刺毛，長可達 8 毫米。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Opposite. Lanceolate to elliptic-lanceolate. Base obtuse to rounded. Apex acuminate. Margin entire. Strigose on both surfaces.

對生。披針形至橢圓狀披針形。基部鈍至圓。先端漸尖。全緣。兩面被糙伏毛。

Size大小

Petiole 1–2.5(–4) cm. Blade (4.5–)8–15(–22) × (1.7–)2.5–5(–8) cm.

葉柄 1–2.5(–4) 厘米。葉片 (4.5–)8–15(–22) × (1.7–)2.5–5(–8) 厘米。

Venation脈

Five-nerved: midvein plus two pairs of lateral primary veins.

5 條基出脈：中脈加兩對側生主脈。

Flowers花

Appearance外觀

Terminal, usually 5-merous, solitary or 3–5 in cymes. Petals purple, broadly obovate, apex emarginate.

頂生，通常 5 數，單生或 3–5 朵組成聚傘花序。花瓣紫色，闊倒卵形，先端微凹。

Stamens雄蕊

10, rarely 8 or 12, dimorphic.

10 枚，罕為 8 或 12，二型。

Hypanthium花被管

Calyx tube 1–2 cm, densely covered with spreading red bristles.

萼管長 1–2 厘米，密被開展的紅色刺毛。

Fruit果

Type類型

Fleshy capsule, 1.5–2.2 × 1.5–2 cm, dehiscing irregularly at maturity.

肉質蒴果，1.5–2.2 × 1.5–2 厘米，成熟時不規則開裂。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2 出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

  • Habitat. Disturbed forests, streamsides, thickets on low hills, roadsides.生境。 受干擾的森林、溪邊、低丘灌叢、路旁。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong.狀況。 原生。香港常見。
  • Separator. Long spreading bristles on the stem and hypanthium, not the appressed scales of Melastoma malabathricum.區別。 莖和花被管具長而開展的刺毛，不是野牡丹那種貼伏的鱗片。

Representative photographs代表照片

Erect shrub on a trail, among ferns.小徑上的直立灌木，生於芒萁之間。

Source出處

Hong Kong Herbarium & South China Botanical Garden (Eds.). (2008). Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌] (Vol. 2). Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.
