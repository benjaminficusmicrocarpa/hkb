BLP 47144Fabaceae

Peltophorum tonkinense

銀珠

(Pierre) Gagnep.

Plantae/Tracheophyta/angiosperm 被子植物/Magnoliopsida 木蘭綱/mesangiosperms 中生被子植物/eudicots 真雙子葉植物/core eudicots 核心真雙子葉植物/superrosids 超薔薇類/rosids 薔薇類/fabids 豆類/Fabales 豆目/Fabaceae 豆科/Peltophorum 盾柱木屬/Peltophorum tonkinense 銀珠

Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 2出處：《香港植物誌》，第二卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Tree, 10–20 m. Young branches and the inflorescence densely rusty-hairy, later nearly hairless. Old branches with many rusty lenticels.

喬木，高 10–20 米。幼枝和花序密被鏽色毛，後來近無毛。老枝有許多鏽色皮孔。

Leaves葉

Leaflets小葉

Bipinnate, the leaf 15–35 cm. Pinnae 6–13 pairs. Leaflets 5–14 pairs, sessile, leathery, oblong, 1.5–2 × 0.6–1 cm. Base narrowed and unequal. Apex obtuse, notched or with a small point.

二回羽狀複葉，長 15–35 厘米。羽片 6–13 對。小葉 5–14 對，無柄，革質，長圓形，1.5–2 × 0.6–1 厘米。基部變狹，不對稱。先端鈍、微凹或有小短尖。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Racemes almost terminal, 8–12 cm. Flower buds globose, about 8 mm across. Calyx lobes ovate, 8–9 mm, densely rusty-hairy. Petals obovate, 1.5 cm, rusty-hairy along the midvein.

總狀花序近頂生，長 8–12 厘米。花蕾球形，直徑約 8 毫米。萼裂片卵形，長 8–9 毫米，密被鏽色毛。花瓣倒卵形，長 1.5 厘米，沿中脈被鏽色毛。

Fruit果

Pod reddish brown when ripe, spindle-shaped, 8–13 × 2.5–3 cm, hairless, winged along both sutures. The wings are 5–7 mm wide.

莢果成熟時紅褐色，紡錘形，8–13 × 2.5–3 厘米，無毛，兩邊的縫線有翅。翅寬 5–7 毫米。
