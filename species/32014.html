Arundina graminifolia

竹葉蘭

(D. Don) Hochr.

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Asparagales
Family科Orchidaceae
Genus屬Arundina
Species種Arundina graminifolia

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Terrestrial herb. Stems erect, grass-like and stiff, leafy. Basal portion swelling into a small ovoid pseudobulb covered by overlapping brown tough scales.

地生草本。莖直立，似草而堅硬，具葉。基部膨大成小的卵形假鱗莖，被覆瓦狀褐色堅韌鱗片。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Linear-lanceolate, thinly leathery or papery, 5–20 × 0.6 cm. Apex acute.

線狀披針形，薄革質或紙質，5–20 × 0.6 厘米。先端急尖。

Sheath葉鞘

Amplexicaul and overlapping, 2–4 cm, articulate at the apical end.

抱莖而覆疊，長 2–4 厘米，頂端有關節。

Flowers花

Inflorescence花序

Terminal, racemose or paniculate. Flowers resupinate.

頂生，總狀或圓錐狀。花扭轉，唇瓣位於下方。

Sepals萼片

Elliptic or elliptic-lanceolate, 2.5–4 × 0.7–0.9 cm, apex acute, pale rose-mauve.

橢圓形或橢圓狀披針形，2.5–4 × 0.7–0.9 厘米，先端急尖，淡玫瑰紫色。

Petals花瓣

Elliptic or ovate-elliptic, 2.5–3.5 × 1.3–1.8 cm, rose-mauve with darker median veins.

橢圓形或卵狀橢圓形，2.5–3.5 × 1.3–1.8 厘米，玫瑰紫色，中脈顏色較深。

Lip唇瓣

Trumpet-shaped, 3–4 cm, embracing the column, wavy at the margin, deeply cleft at the apex, pale magenta deepening to bright rose-purple towards the margin and apex.

喇叭形，長 3–4 厘米，抱蕊柱，邊緣波狀，先端深裂，淡洋紅色，向邊緣及先端加深為亮玫瑰紫色。

Column蕊柱

Slender, curved, slightly winged at the apical end. Pollinia 8, discoid.

細長，彎曲，頂端稍具翅。花粉塊 8，盤狀。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Grassy slopes or stream banks in exposed areas.生境。 開曠處的草坡，或溪岸。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong.狀況。 原生。香港常見。
  • Distribution. Central, east, south and southwest China; tropical Asia.分布。 中國中、東、南及西南；熱帶亞洲。

Representative photographs代表照片

A clump in a rock crevice, with grass-like leaves, two open pale rose-mauve flowers and buds.石縫中的一叢，葉似草，兩朵開放的淡玫瑰紫色花及花蕾。

Source出處

Hong Kong Herbarium & South China Botanical Garden (Eds.). (2011). Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌] (Vol. 4). Agriculture, Fisheries and Conservation Department.
