BLP 55350Native原生Hypericaceae

Cratoxylum cochinchinense

黃牛木

(Lour.) Blume

Home首頁 Species Directory物種目錄 Cratoxylum cochinchinenseBLP 55350

Taxonomic classification (APG IV)分類（APG IV）

Order目Malpighiales
Family科Hypericaceae
Genus屬Cratoxylum
Species種Cratoxylum cochinchinense

Plant morphology植物形態

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Shrubs or trees, 1–10 m tall. Bark yellowish white, glabrous.

灌木或喬木，高 1–10 米。樹皮黃白色，無毛。

Leaves葉

Shape形狀

Petiole 2–5 mm. Blade elliptic, narrow-elliptic, ovate-oblong or rarely subovate, 3–11 × 1.5–4 cm. Base cuneate, rarely rounded. Apex acute, shortly acuminate or slightly obtuse. Lateral veins slightly obvious on both surfaces.

葉柄 2–5 毫米。葉片橢圓形、狹橢圓形、卵狀長圓形，稀近卵形，3–11 × 1.5–4 厘米。基部楔形，稀圓形。先端急尖、短漸尖或稍鈍。側脈兩面稍明顯。

Flowers and fruit花與果

Flowers花

Red, ca. 1 cm in diam. Cymes axillary or extra-axillary, 1–5-flowered. Stamens usually united into 3 bundles, 4–8 mm. Glands 3, alternate with the staminal bundles, conic, 2–3 mm.

紅色，直徑約 1 厘米。聚傘花序腋生或腋外生，有花 1–5 朵。雄蕊通常合生為 3 束，長 4–8 毫米。腺體 3，與雄蕊束互生，圓錐狀，2–3 毫米。

Capsule蒴果

Elliptic, 8–12 mm, with a persistent calyx. Seeds 6 mm, winged on one side.

橢圓形，8–12 毫米，具宿存萼。種子 6 毫米，一側有翅。

Ecology生態

  • Habitat. Thickets or secondary forests.生境。 灌叢或次生林。
  • Status. Native. Common in Hong Kong.狀況。 原生。香港常見。
  • Distribution. Guangdong, Guangxi and southern Yunnan; Indochina, Thailand, Myanmar, the Philippines and Indonesia.分布。 廣東、廣西及雲南南部；中南半島、泰國、緬甸、菲律賓及印度尼西亞。

Representative photographs代表照片
