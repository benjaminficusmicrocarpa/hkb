Plant morphology植物形態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1 出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷

Habit習性

Growth form生長形態

Small tree, all parts strongly camphor-scented; branchlets angular, glabrous or minutely tomentulose near apex.

小喬木，全株有濃樟香；小枝有棱，無毛，或近先端被微絨毛。

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Leaves alternate; petioles 0.8 –1.3 cm, glabrous; leaf blade thick-leathery, elliptic, 4–9.5 × 2–3.5 cm, base cuneate, apex abruptly and shortly acuminate, tip blunt, dark green and shining adaxially, paler and reddish abaxially, triplinerved; midvein and lateral veins slightly impressed adaxially and strongly raised abaxially, transversal veins inconspicuous.

葉互生；葉柄長 0.8–1.3 厘米，無毛；葉片厚革質，橢圓形，長 4–9.5 厘米，寬 2–3.5 厘米，基部楔形，先端驟然短漸尖，尖頭鈍，上面深綠色而有光澤，下面較淺並帶紅，離基三出脈；中脈和側脈在上面稍下陷、在下面明顯隆起，橫脈不明顯。

Flowers花

Inflorescence / flower花序／花

Panicles about the length of the substanding leaves, cymosely branching, the utmost branch 3-flowered. Pedicels very short, greyish white sericeous.

圓錐花序約與所對的葉等長，聚傘狀分枝，最末級分枝有花 3 朵。花梗極短，被灰白色絹毛。

Fruit果

Fruit果

Fruit unknown.

果實未見。

Ecology生態

Source: Flora of Hong Kong [香港植物誌], Vol. 1 出處：《香港植物誌》，第一卷
