Growth form生長形態

Small tree, all parts strongly camphor-scented; branchlets angular, glabrous or minutely tomentulose near apex.

Leaves葉

Blade葉片

Leaves alternate; petioles 0.8 –1.3 cm, glabrous; leaf blade thick-leathery, elliptic, 4–9.5 × 2–3.5 cm, base cuneate, apex abruptly and shortly acuminate, tip blunt, dark green and shining adaxially, paler and reddish abaxially, triplinerved; midvein and lateral veins slightly impressed adaxially and strongly raised abaxially, transversal veins inconspicuous.

（譯稿待核）Leaves alternate; petioles 0.8 –1.3 厘米, 無毛; leaf blade thick-leathery, elliptic, 4–9.5 × 2–3.5 厘米, base cuneate, apex abruptly and shortly acuminate, tip blunt, dark green and shining adaxially, paler and reddish abaxially, triplinerved; midvein and lateral veins slightly impressed adaxially and strongly raised abaxially, transversal veins inconspicuous.

（譯稿待核）Panicles about the length of the substanding leaves, cymosely branching, the utmost branch 3-flowered. Pedicels very short, greyish white sericeous.
